Flowers & waves

Choose a tile, then Plant & pulse. Later, choose an existing flower to pulse it for free. Every connected flower lights once. Link flowers pass the pulse to neighbors directly above, below, left or right; Bloom flowers receive it but do not pass it on.

Each lit flower deals 1 damage within its reach, measured by tile steps without diagonals. A gloom can be hit by several flowers. The bright tiles show reach before you commit. Numbers on gloom show its remaining strength.

After the pulse, surviving gloom moves one tile left. New gloom then appears at the right edge. Flowers do not block movement. Each gloom reaching the left edge costs one heart. Protect at least one heart until the wave ends; keeping all three is a stronger finish.

There is no timer. Undo restores the whole turn, including seeds and hearts. Reset is also undoable. Four authored gardens introduce branching links and wide blooms. Progress resets on reload; no data is saved or uploaded.
